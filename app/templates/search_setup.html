Job Search Setup

AJAS uses JSearch to find jobs from LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, Google Jobs, and more — covering any city in any country. It is completely free.
Step 1 — Create a free RapidAPI account

JSearch runs on RapidAPI. Click below to sign up for a free account. No credit card required.

Sign up at RapidAPI →
Step 2 — Subscribe to JSearch (free)

After signing in, open the JSearch API page and click Subscribe to Test. Select the Basic plan — it is free and gives you 200 searches per month.

Open JSearch on RapidAPI →
Step 3 — Find your API key

On the JSearch page, scroll down to the Code Snippet section. You will see a line labelled "X-RapidAPI-Key" with a long string of letters and numbers. That is your key. Copy it exactly.
